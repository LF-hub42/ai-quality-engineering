# Case Studies

This section documents development contributions and later reviews of available project evidence. The case studies distinguish my work, AI-assisted contributions and what the available records can verify. Simulated assessments are documented separately.

## Purpose

Case studies record engineering challenges, my involvement, approaches, available evidence and results when they can be verified.

They remain separate from the practice assessments and simulated scenarios in this repository.

## Documentation Standards

Each case study should cover:

1. **Context:** The project background and problem.
2. **Challenges:** Identified risks, requirements and limitations.
3. **Contribution:** My direct work and the role of AI tools or coding agents.
4. **Verification:** Source-defined coverage, historical results and checks performed for the later review.
5. **Outcomes:** Supported results and lessons learned.
6. **Transparency:** Clear distinction between project development and simulated assessments.
