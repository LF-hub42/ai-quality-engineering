# Assessments

These five ChatGPT assessments document my practice evaluating AI-generated behavior, identifying risks and communicating quality findings. The scenarios are simulations, not production incidents or independently certified examinations.

## Assessment Overview

1. [Assessment 001: Introductory AI Evaluation Exercises](assessment-001-introductory-ai-evaluation/README.md)
   Five introductory exercises in structured evaluation.
2. [Assessment 002: Mixed AI Quality Evaluation](assessment-002-mixed-ai-quality-evaluation/README.md)
   Ten exercises covering mixed quality dimensions.
3. [Assessment 003: AI Agent Quality Assurance](assessment-003-ai-agent-quality-assurance/README.md)
   Ten agent-oriented scenario QA exercises. This was not an autonomous production deployment.
4. [Assessment 004: Advanced AI Quality Evaluation](assessment-004-advanced-ai-quality-evaluation/README.md)
   Ten advanced exercises focused on risk, evidence and governance.
5. [Assessment 005: Scenario-Based AI Agent Evaluation](assessment-005-real-world-ai-agent-evaluation/README.md)
   Five simulated scenarios with German evaluations and English translations.

## Evidence Scope

Assessments 001–004 are historical summaries. Their original prompts, individual responses and detailed reviewer feedback are not available in this repository. I have not reconstructed them. Assessment 005 includes edited German evaluations, English translations and condensed scenario descriptions complete original prompts and reviewer transcripts are not included.
