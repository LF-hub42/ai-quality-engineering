## Evaluation Objectives

The exercises focused on three initial goals:

1. Establish a structured approach to evaluating AI-generated behavior.
2. Practice identifying potential quality risks and documenting findings.
3. Develop consistency in writing clear, actionable evaluation notes.

These objectives formed the foundation for the more detailed assessment documentation developed later.

## Assessment Context

The exercises were conducted through standard ChatGPT conversations.

Scenarios were generated by AI, while the evaluations were written by me.

Feedback was provided through AI-generated reviewer responses.

Any incident-related scenarios were simulated and did not represent actual production incidents.

## Evidence and Limitations

This page is a historical summary, not a complete evidence package. The repository does not include the original scenario prompts, individual evaluations, detailed findings, severity classifications, reviewer feedback or scoring rubric. No individual responses or results have been reconstructed.

Assessment 001 marks the beginning of my structured approach to AI quality evaluation. The focus was on developing a repeatable method, which later assessments documented in greater detail.
