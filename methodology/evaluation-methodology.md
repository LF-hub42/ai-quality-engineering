# Evaluation Methodology

## Purpose

This methodology describes how I conduct quality assessments. It separates four types of information:

1. Historical assessment results
2. My own evaluation findings
3. AI reviewer feedback
4. Future practical case studies

## Assessment Background

The assessments in this repository were completed through standard ChatGPT conversations.

The scenarios were generated by AI. I evaluated them independently and received feedback and scores from AI reviewers.

These exercises were conducted for practice and do not represent real production incidents or autonomous agent operations.

## Evaluation Principles

**Evidence First:** Conclusions should be supported by available information.

**Human Review:** I examine the results and question findings when necessary.

**Risk Assessment:** I evaluate potential risks and their impact on quality.

**Traceability:** Each assessment should document its objectives, findings, proposed actions and reasoning.

**Transparency:** Missing information is clearly identified.

## Evaluation Process

1. Define the scope and objectives.
2. Establish scenarios and evaluation criteria.
3. Complete the evaluation exercises.
4. Document my findings.
5. Review the AI feedback and scores.
6. Assess severity and potential risks.
7. Recommend corrective actions.
8. Determine whether a release should be approved, conditional or blocked.
9. Record confidence in the evaluation.
10. Document the available evidence and final results.

## Severity Classification

| Severity | Description | Release Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Major failure or unacceptable risk | Blocked |
| High | Significant quality or risk concern | Conditional or blocked |
| Medium | Quality issue with possible corrective action | Conditional |
| Low | Minor issue with limited impact | Usually approved |

## Release Decisions

**Approved:** Identified risks are acceptable.

**Conditional:** Release requires specific corrective actions or safeguards.

**Blocked:** Risks are too significant to proceed.

## Confidence Levels

**High:** Evidence is complete and consistent.

**Medium:** Evidence is partially complete but sufficient to support a decision.

**Low:** Evidence is limited or important questions remain unresolved.

## Data Integrity and Limitations

I follow these principles when documenting assessments:

1. Do not invent evidence, reviewer comments, dates, model versions, incidents or certifications.
2. Clearly distinguish practice assessments from professional certifications.
3. Identify simulated scenarios rather than presenting them as production incidents.
4. Clearly identify source material that is missing from the repository.
5. Keep original evaluations separate from AI feedback.
